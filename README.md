MEng Mechanical Engineering student at Swansea University (Sep 2025 – Jun 2030). I design, build and test hardware: mechanical design and CAD, PCBs and embedded firmware.

## Projects

**Type 0 CNC Mill** (Nov 2026 – present)
Designing a 3-axis CNC mill for aluminium, brass and steel: 400 × 250 × 150 mm work envelope, a ±0.02 mm part accuracy target and a £4,200 Phase 1 budget. A moving steel box-section gantry on a cast epoxy granite tub, with HGR20 rails, C5 ballscrews and closed-loop steppers. I am modelling the machine in Fusion, sizing the gantry, screws and motors, sourcing the BOM, and designing the electrical cabinet with LinuxCNC on a Mesa 7I76EU.

**Ergonomic Keyboard Modules – client commission** (Nov 2025 – present)
Reverse-engineered Xbox and GameCube button mechanisms. Designed module and daughterboard PCBs in KiCad to work with the client's existing keyboard, and wrote the firmware in embedded C. The modules connect with JST connectors to suit different hand sizes.

**IMechE Pipe Climber 2026** (Jan 2026 – Jun 2026)
An autonomous robot that climbs a 2.2 m pipe and lifts a chain, with analogue control only. Team of six. I designed the acrylic and PLA chassis around a removable motor and battery cassette, and produced all the CAD in Fusion.
